Frequently asked questions

Your questions about German courses at DeFI.

How long does it take to learn German A1 to C1 at DeFI?

Most students complete A1 to B2 in 12 to 18 months with regular attendance. C1 preparation typically takes an additional 3 to 6 months depending on your starting level and study pace. DeFI in Yaoundé offers a progressive curriculum designed to build your German skills step by step.

Can DeFI help me get a student visa for Germany?

DeFI provides language training and orientation to help you prepare your academic and visa documents. We do not guarantee visa outcomes, but our guidance helps you understand the process clearly. Many of our students in Yaoundé have successfully used DeFI preparation as part of their Germany study application.

What is Ausbildung and does DeFI prepare students for it?

Ausbildung is Germany's dual vocational training system, combining classroom learning with hands-on work experience. DeFI helps students in Yaoundé understand the Ausbildung pathway, build the required German language level (usually B1–B2), and prepare for the application process with clear orientation.

Is DeFI a registered language school in Yaoundé?

DeFI (Deutsch-Freunde Institut) is a language institute based in Simbock/Mendong, Yaoundé, Cameroon. We offer structured German courses from A1 to C1 and pathway orientation for students heading to Germany. You can find us at Entrée Simbock, behind Santa Monica Bakery.

Do I need any prior German knowledge to start at DeFI?

No. DeFI offers courses for complete beginners starting at A1 level. Our progressive curriculum takes you from zero knowledge to advanced German step by step. Many of our current students started with no German at all.
